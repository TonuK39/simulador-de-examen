Storage Manager – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026 · App: Storage Manager (com.hinojosa.storagemanager)

Storage Manager helps you understand and free up space on your Android device. This policy explains what information the app accesses and what it does with it. In short: everything stays on your device. The app does not collect, store on any server, sell or share your personal data.

Information the app accesses (on your device only)

  • Files and media (photos, videos, audio, documents, downloads) — to show storage usage, thumbnails, large files and duplicates, and to delete the items you choose. With “All files access” the app also scans shared storage folders to find leftover files (installer APKs, thumbnail caches, temporary/log files and empty folders).
  • App usage and storage statistics (Usage access) — to show each app’s size, data and cache, and which apps you have not opened in a while.
  • List of installed apps — to build the App manager and let you open, inspect or uninstall apps.
  • Battery level and storage totals — shown in the app and in the home screen widget.

All of this is processed locally on your device. None of it is transmitted to the developer or to third parties.

Deleting files

The app only deletes files when you confirm it, or — if you turn on the Pro “Scheduled cleaning” feature — it automatically removes only leftover junk (installer APKs, thumbnail caches, temporary/log files and empty folders). It never deletes photos, videos, documents or app data on its own.

Purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is sold through Google Play. Payments are processed by Google; the app never sees your payment details. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play that the purchase exists, to unlock Pro features. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows the result of scheduled cleaning and low-storage alerts. These are generated on the device.

Data collection, analytics and ads

The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking SDKs, and it does not create accounts.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
